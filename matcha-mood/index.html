Our story

A small cart with a big mood.

Matcha Mood is a mobile matcha cart serving Dallas Fort Worth. We bring the cart, the whisks and a menu built around your celebration, so every cup feels like part of the décor.

From weddings to birthdays to graduations, we pour iced matcha drinks your guests will want to photograph before the first sip.
