Our story

A little cart with a big pour.

Phylla began with a love of good matcha and a simple thought: the best cup is the one made right in front of you. So we built a cart, dressed it in cream and green, and took the whisk on the road.

From candlelit wedding receptions to boutique launches on London high streets, we arrive, set up beautifully and whisk every drink fresh. You enjoy the moment. We look after the matcha.
