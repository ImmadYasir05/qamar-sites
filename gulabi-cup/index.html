Our story

A little pink cup with a big heart.

Gulabi Cup is a mobile matcha cart from New York City, blending ceremonial matcha with the flavors we grew up with. Gulabi means pink, and every cup carries a little of that warmth.

We bring the cart, the whisks and the menu to weddings, private events and pop ups, so your guests get a drink that feels like a celebration of its own.
