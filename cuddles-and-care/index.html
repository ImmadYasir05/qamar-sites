Meet Mimi

A trusted pair of hands with a big heart.

Hi parents! I'm Mimi, a proudly professional nanny offering a confident pair of hands you can trust while you focus on the many parts of a busy, growing family life.

I care not just about the quality of time spent with your children, but about the quality of experience they carry into their future. I hold a Certificate III and a Diploma in Early Childhood Education and Care, and I'm currently studying a Bachelor of Early Childhood and Primary Teaching.

with love, Mimi
