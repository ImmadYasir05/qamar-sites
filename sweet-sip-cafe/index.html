Our story

A small cart with a big sweet tooth.

Sweet Sip Cafe is a mobile café from Woodbridge, pouring South Asian inspired drinks made with love. Think rich Thai tea, smooth mango lassi and warm chai, next to iced lattes and matcha layered with flavors like salted caramel crème brûlée, tiramisu and campfire.

You will find our pink cart at markets, festivals and summer concerts. We also bring it to baby showers and private celebrations, with bottled drinks your guests can take home. Everything we serve is halal.
