Our story

A little cart with a big table.

The Cake Club started with a simple idea: dessert should be part of the moment, not an afterthought. So we built a cart, filled it with generous trays of cake and brought it straight to the party.

From candlelit receptions to intimate engagements, we roll in, set up beautifully and serve every guest fresh from the tray. You enjoy your night. We look after the sweet part.
