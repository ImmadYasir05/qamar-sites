Our story

A small studio with a big eye for detail.

Decor by Shama & Bushra began with a love for turning a room into a moment. Every setup starts with a conversation about your colors, your traditions and the feeling you want your guests to walk into.

From rich, regal bridal stages to soft pastel florals and joyful mehndi color, each design is made for one event only: yours.

with love, Shama & Bushra
