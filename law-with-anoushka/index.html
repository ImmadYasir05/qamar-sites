About

Same law, less confusion.

The law shapes almost everything around us, yet it is usually explained in a language most people never learned. Law with Anoushka exists to change that, one clear and honest video at a time.

Each week, Anoushka breaks down Canadian law, the political moments behind the headlines and the questions people are too shy to ask. No jargon, no lecture. Just the law, made simple, relevant and real.
