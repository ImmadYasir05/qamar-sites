Meet Noori

Real science for real lives.

Noori holds an MSc in Nutrition and Global Health and is a Registered Associate Nutritionist with the Association for Nutrition. She is also a qualified personal trainer, with four years of experience coaching calisthenics and strength training for women of every level.

On Instagram she turns the latest research into plain, practical advice, from cycle awareness to the role of fibre in women's health. In her sessions she brings that same clarity to your goals, so you leave with strategies that fit your routine, your culture and your life.
