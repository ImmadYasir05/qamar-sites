Meet Muriam

A mobile physio with a big belief in independence.

If you are recovering from surgery, living with joint pain, or struggling to get out and about, you do not have to face it alone. Muriam is an HCPC registered physiotherapist with over 9 years of NHS and private experience.

She started out studying Sports Therapy, switched to Physiotherapy after the first year, and has never looked back. Today she brings specialist care directly to you, through home visits in Peterborough and surrounding areas, clinic appointments, or remote consultations worldwide, so you can focus on moving forward without the stress of travelling.
