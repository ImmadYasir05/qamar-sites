Our story

A small booth with a big spin.

SnapLux started with a simple idea: photos freeze a moment, but the best parts of a celebration are the ones that move. The laughter, the twirl, the confetti in the air.

So we built a luxury 360 experience for the GTA. Studio lighting, a sleek platform, custom overlays and videos your guests can share before the night is over. We set up, we run it, we take it all down. You just enjoy the party.
