Our story

A small kitchen with a big spread.

Lovely Bites began with a simple idea: the table should be the moment everyone remembers. Every board and grazing table is built by hand and layered with cheeses, cured meats, fresh fruit, sweets, florals and greenery.

Whether it is a cozy board for a night in or a grazing table that runs the length of the room, each spread is designed around your event, your guests and the little details you love.
