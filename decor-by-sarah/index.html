Our story

A simple room, a grand entrance.

Since 2017, Decor by Sarah has been turning halls, homes and banquet rooms into spaces that take your breath away. Rich velvet drapes, layered florals, candlelight and custom signage, all brought together around your colors and your story.

Whether you want the full design and setup or just the perfect pieces to style yourself, we bring the same care to every detail, from the first sketch to the last candle.
