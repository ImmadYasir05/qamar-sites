Our story

A small bakery with a big blessing.

Hi, I'm Barakah Junaid, and I have been running Baked with Blessings on my own for the last three years. Barakah means blessings in Arabic, and I started with one goal: to bless the people around me and my community with quality baked goods.

I grew up making the craziest concoctions and learning from every show on the Food Network. One thing I rarely saw on TV was a fellow Muslim woman. In a male dominated field, I want to show the talent and skill a Muslim woman is capable of, serving halal and hospitality.

While earning my degrees I became Head Pastry Chef at Four Corners. Now Baked with Blessings is my main focus, and I am so grateful to God, my family, my friends, every supporter, and my siblings for being my free workers.

BarakahFounder & pastry chef
