Our story

A small kitchen with a big sweet tooth.

Khadija's Delights is a homemade bakery in Queens, built on a love of the old fashioned, over the top vintage cake. Think scalloped borders, piped shells, cherries and satin ribbon.

Because every order is made by hand, the calendar fills up fast. Each cake gets the time it deserves, so we take a limited number each week and make every one of them count.
