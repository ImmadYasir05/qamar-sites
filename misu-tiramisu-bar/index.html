Our story

A small cart with a big scoop.

MISÙ started with one simple idea: tiramisu tastes better when it is made for the moment. Every batch is handcrafted by Sarwat, then brought to your event and scooped live, right in front of your guests.

You choose the toppings, we bring the cart, the cups and the care. From weddings and bridal showers to campus nights and community days, MISÙ turns dessert into something people gather around.
