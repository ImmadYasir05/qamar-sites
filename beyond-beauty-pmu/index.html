Meet Veronica

A small studio with a big eye for detail.

Veronica is a permanent makeup artist and trainer in Palm Beach Gardens. She calls herself a brow perfectionist, and every appointment shows it: shapes mapped to your face, pigment chosen for your skin, and results kept soft enough to still look like you.

Detail obsessed, effortless results. That is the whole idea. Alongside her clients, she teaches 1:1 permanent makeup trainings for artists who want to learn the craft properly.
