Terms &
Conditions

1 · Fees and payment

The total fee is $349.00 USD, a flat fee with no taxes or other charges added. It is paid in one payment by Stripe when this agreement is signed, before work begins.

2 · Revisions

Reasonable revisions are included at no extra charge while the project is in progress, as long as they stay within the scope on page 03. New pages or features requested after signing are quoted separately and agreed in writing before work starts.

3 · Client responsibilities

The Client supplies text, photos, logos and feedback within 2 business days of each request, and confirms it has the right to use everything it supplies. Delays beyond that move the delivery date back by the same amount.

4 · Schedule

Work begins Monday, November 2, 2026, once this agreement is signed and the fee is paid. The site is delivered within 2 weeks of that date, provided content and feedback arrive on time. Any change to the timeline is agreed in writing, which includes email.

5 · Ownership

On delivery the Client owns the website, its design and its domain, as set out on page 05. The Studio keeps its own tools, code libraries and pre-existing work, and grants the Client a permanent, free license to use any of them included in the site.

6 · Portfolio rights

The Studio may show the finished website in its portfolio and case studies unless the Client asks otherwise in writing.

7 · Confidentiality

Both parties keep non-public business information private and use it only to carry out this agreement. This continues after the project ends.

8 · Termination

Either party may end this agreement with 7 days written notice. If the Client ends it, the Studio keeps 25% of the fee for each stage on page 03 that has started and refunds the rest. If the Studio ends it, it refunds the fee for every stage not yet completed. Either way the Client receives the files completed so far.

9 · Warranty and liability

The Studio does the work with reasonable skill and care. Beyond the commitments in this agreement, the site is provided as is. Each party's total liability is limited to the fee paid under this agreement, and neither is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

10 · Governing law and general

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law rules. Disputes go first to good-faith discussion, then to mediation. The parties are independent contractors. Notices and changes must be in writing, and email counts. If any term is unenforceable, the rest still applies. Electronic signatures are binding.

Ownership &
Support

11 · Ownership transfer

During the build the Client gets a private preview link, so progress can be checked at any time. On delivery the Client owns everything built for it: the full source code, the front end (design and content) and the back end (hosting account and any content management system). The Studio transfers every account and login to the Client within 2 business days of delivery. The Client is free to edit the code or hire any developer, with no lock-in to the Studio.

12 · Domain registration

The domain is registered in the Client's name and its first year of registration is included in the fee. The Client owns the domain and can move it to its own registrar account at any time, and the Studio handles that transfer free.

13 · Future updates and fees

Anything in the original build that stops working is fixed free for as long as the site is live and the Studio is operating. Small updates, such as swapping text, photos, prices or services, are also free. Larger work, such as new pages or features, gets a fixed-price quote in writing first. Nothing starts until the Client approves it, and the Client never pays more than the approved quote. The Client is never required to use the Studio for updates.
