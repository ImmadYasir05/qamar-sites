Our story

A small studio with a big love of celebrating.

Studio Ri Co. began in Michigan with one simple idea: the moments worth celebrating deserve details made just for them. A name in the right colors. A theme that feels like the person of honor. A banner that makes everyone stop and smile when they walk in.

Every order starts with a conversation. You tell us what you are celebrating, share your vision, and we work together to bring your idea to life.
