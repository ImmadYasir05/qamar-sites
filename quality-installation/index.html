Our story

A small crew with a big standard.

Quality Installation is a hands on tile company led by Alexander Timkin. We take on showers, floors, backsplashes and full bathroom remodels, and we show up for every one of them ourselves.

The work you never see matters most to us: a solid substrate, careful waterproofing and a layout planned before the first tile goes down. That is what keeps the lines straight and the finish lasting.
