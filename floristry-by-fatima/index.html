The story

a small studio with a big heart.

Floristry by Fatima is one person, one pair of hands and a lot of care. Every order starts with your inspiration: the colors, the flowers, the size and the feeling you want someone to have when they hold it.

From a red rose bouquet with a single initial in baby's breath to a soft blush birthday bunch with orchids and a little card tucked in the ribbon, each piece is made for one person and one moment. That is the whole point.

How to order

three steps to your flowers.

01

send your inspiration

Two or three inspiration photos or a description of what you have in mind. Colors, flowers, size and overall style are always helpful.

02

date and time

Let Fatima know the date and the exact time you need your order for. Custom orders need at least 4 days notice.

03

pickup or delivery

Collect from Teaneck at your agreed time, or have it delivered. Delivery fees depend on location and are discussed when you order.
