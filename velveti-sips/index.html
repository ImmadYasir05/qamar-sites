Our story

A small bar with a big pour.

Velveti Sips is an artisan drink bar from Staten Island. Coffee, matcha and refreshers, each one made by hand with real attention to the little things: the layers, the garnish, the way it looks in your hand.

You can order for pickup in 10305, or find us at markets and pop ups around the island. Either way, every cup gets the same care.
