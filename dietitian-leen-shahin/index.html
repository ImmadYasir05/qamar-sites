Meet Leen

A nutritionist with a big love for food.

Leen trained in clinical nutrition and dietetics, and she loves talking about nutrition, health and exercise. She also loves food, which is exactly why her approach works: no banned lists, no all or nothing rules, just a balanced lifestyle you can actually keep.

On Instagram she breaks down the things people really ask about, from bloating and sleep to cholesterol numbers and insulin resistance, in plain words and in Arabic. Her consultations bring that same clarity to your own goals.
