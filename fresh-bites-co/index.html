Our story

Small bites with a big welcome.

Fresh Bites Co believes the food table should be the prettiest thing in the room. Every spread is built in layers, with individual cups, baskets of fruit, trays of savoury bites and florals, so your guests reach for their cameras before their plates.

We are Food Handler Certified and cater across the GTA, with halal, vegetarian and gluten free options. Birthdays, graduations and family celebrations: tell us the occasion and we will take care of the table.
