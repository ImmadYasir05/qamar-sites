Our story

A home kitchen with a big sweet tooth.

Dipped by Nina is a home bakery in Howell, New Jersey, making stuffed cookies, cake pops and chocolate dipped treats for the cravings that will not wait.

Every season brings a new lineup, from caramel apple and German chocolate turtle in the fall to a spooky cast of cookies for Halloween. Order ahead, pick up fresh, and share if you really have to.

xo, Nina
