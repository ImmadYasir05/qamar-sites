        Our story

        Barakah means blessings.

        Hi, I'm Barakah Junaid. For the last three years I have run Baked with Blessings on my own, with one goal: to bless the people around me and my community with quality baked goods.

        I grew up making the craziest concoctions and learning from every Food Network show. What I rarely saw was a fellow Muslim woman. In a male dominated field, I want to show what a Muslim woman can do, serving halal and hospitality.

        While earning my degrees I became Head Pastry Chef at Four Corners. Now this bakery is my main focus, and I'm grateful to God, my family, my supporters, and my siblings for being my free workers.

        Barakah JunaidFounder & pastry chef
