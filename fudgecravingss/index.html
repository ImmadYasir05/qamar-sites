Our story

A small brand with a big sweet tooth.

Fudgecravingss is a custom fudge brownie brand from Liverpool in Sydney, built on one simple promise: delight in every bite.

Pick a box to share, send a heart shaped slab in any theme, or keep a little tin all to yourself. Tell us what you are craving and we will make it yours, right down to the toppings.
