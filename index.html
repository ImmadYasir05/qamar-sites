Our story

A small bar with a big pour.

BrewTeaful began with one bowl, one whisk and a love for matcha done properly. Every cup starts with first harvest matcha, whisked by hand right in front of your guests, then finished with syrups we make ourselves.

From bridal suites to office floors to Saturday markets, we set up a bar that looks as good as it tastes, so the matcha becomes part of the memory.
