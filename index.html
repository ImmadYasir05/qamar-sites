Our story

Every ramo, made by Jess.

RamossbyJess started with a love for big, full rose bouquets and the moment someone opens the door to one. Every ramo is wrapped by hand in Los Angeles, in the colors you choose, for the person you're celebrating.

Birthdays, anniversaries, graduations or just because: tell us who it's for and we'll make it feel like them, right down to the ribbon and the card.

con mucho amor, Jess
