Meet the baker

A small kitchen with a big heart.

Hi, I'm Lenicia. Baker Benzz started with a love for turning a simple idea into something people remember. A colour, a theme, a little inside joke: you bring the story and I'll bring it to life in sponge, buttercream and sugar.

From soft ivory wedding tiers to dramatic black rose cakes and handmade fondant characters, every order is designed just for you and finished by hand, down to the last piped pearl.

with love, Lenicia
