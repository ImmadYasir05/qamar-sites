Our story

A small cafe with a big love for matcha.

Matcha On Ice started with one simple idea: matcha should taste as good as it looks. We serve first harvest matcha, the youngest and smoothest leaves of the season, and build every drink around it.

Beyond the counter, we bring matcha to you. Our cart pours at weddings, brand launches and wellness mornings across San Diego, and our community hosts slow, beautiful wellness events through @matchaonice_.
