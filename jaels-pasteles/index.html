Meet the baker

A little kitchen with a big heart.

Hi, I'm Jael. Every cake that leaves my kitchen is made to order for one celebration: yours. Vintage piping, ruffled hearts, bold birthday colors, soft florals, whatever you dream up, I build it by hand.

From first birthdays to milestone thirties, from a sweet little bento to a statement tier, the goal is the same. A cake that looks unforgettable and tastes like home. Hablamos español, so tell me your idea in whichever language feels right.

con amor, Jael
