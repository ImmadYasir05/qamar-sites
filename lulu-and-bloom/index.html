Our story

Small studio, big blooms.

Lulu & Bloom is a Sydney florist built on one idea: flowers should feel generous. Our baskets overflow, our bouquets are full and every piece is finished with our ribbon, ready to be gifted.

Nothing sits on a shelf. Every order is made fresh for you, whether it is a birthday, an apology, a thank you or simply a Tuesday that needs a little colour.
